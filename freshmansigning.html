On June 13, 2025 the upcoming freshman football team members and their families gathered to officially sign as Bath County High School Wildcats. Attending this 2nd annual Freshman Signing Day were several upcoming Sophomore, Junior and Senior players and their families.


Following the signing presentation, Coach Brewer introduced Seth Miller as the newest addition to his coaching staff and Offensive Coordinator.


Congratulations to these Freshmen and Coach Miller! Welcome to the WILDCAT family!
